The True Value of Money:
Is It Everything?

By: Ahmed Abdullah Zakkour

The question of whether money is important is as old as society itself. Undeniably, money is a fundamental necessity in our modern world. It provides us with basic human needs: food, shelter, healthcare, and education. It offers security and the freedom to make choices, significantly reducing the stress related to basic survival.

However, while money is crucial for a comfortable life, it is not the ultimate source of happiness. Wealth cannot buy genuine friendships, true love, or a peaceful mind. History and daily life show us many individuals who possess great wealth but suffer from loneliness or poor mental health.

People often find the greatest joy in experiences, family relationships, and personal growth rather than in accumulating material possessions. Therefore, money should be viewed as a useful tool to facilitate a good life, not as the final goal of existence.

Is Money Important in Our Lives?

Prepared by: Mohammed Mesfer Al-Massari Al-Qahtani

In today’s fast-paced world, the importance of money cannot be ignored. Money is the medium that allows us to secure our basic necessities, such as a safe place to live, nutritious food, and access to quality healthcare. Without a certain level of financial stability, individuals face constant hardship and anxiety regarding their daily survival.

Beyond basic needs, money provides opportunities. It allows us to pursue higher education, travel, and experience different cultures. It acts as a safety net during unexpected emergencies, giving families a sense of security and peace of mind.

Nevertheless, it is essential to recognize the limits of wealth. Money can purchase a comfortable bed, but not a good night's sleep; it can buy medicine, but not health. True contentment comes from strong family bonds, a sense of purpose, and inner peace. Money is an important passenger in the journey of life, but it should never be the driver.

Yahya Mesfer Al-Massari Al-Qahtani

Money is universally recognized as a critical component of modern existence. It is the tool we use to navigate society, exchange value, and secure our basic standard of living. Having enough money eliminates the desperation associated with poverty and allows individuals to focus on personal development rather than mere survival.

Is Money the Key to Happiness?

It is a common saying that "money makes the world go round." Indeed, financial stability is crucial in the modern era. Money provides access to resources that enhance our quality of life. It allows us to live comfortably, pursue our passions, and protect our families from unforeseen crises.

Without money, people often face severe limitations and daily struggles. Therefore, striving for financial success through education and hard work is a logical and necessary ambition. It gives individuals a platform to contribute to the economy and support their communities.

Despite its importance, money cannot be considered the sole measure of a successful life. True wealth is found in physical health, supportive friends, and a loving family. While money can buy convenience, it cannot buy character or respect. A wise person respects money for the security it provides, but values humanity, ethics, and relationships above all else.
